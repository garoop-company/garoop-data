## Introduction
I'm Daiki Yamashita, CEO of Garoop Inc. (Garoop). Garoop is a kangaroo company. Today I'd like to think a little about "AI and depression," and about the hypothesis of "what logical thinking exists for."

## What Is Logical Thinking?
I have a hypothesis. It is the idea that **"logical thinking exists to keep people from becoming ill."** I think this is more or less correct. For people, the pattern of "acting because of a reason" is close to zero. In reality, the "action" comes first, and the "reasoning" is just added afterward.

* Human actions are not taken because of reasons; reasons are often attached after the action
* Logical thinking exists to keep people from becoming ill
* When it comes to AI and depression as well, logical thinking is important
