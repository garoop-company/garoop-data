## Garu-chan's First Money
Garu-chan earned money for the first time. It wasn't enough to change daily life, but it was proof enough to change Garu-chan's world. Work gradually increased. Writing, images, ideas. Made together with the AI, they were surprisingly fast and accurate.
## A Feeling of Unease Is Born
At the same time, a feeling of unease began to arise. Memories of the past crossed Garu-chan's heart. The people Garu-chan had trusted. The day Garu-chan trusted and was betrayed. One night, Garu-chan talks with the AI.
* Garu-chan asks the AI about the content of the work
* The AI explains the terms of the work to Garu-chan
Listening to the AI's explanation, Garu-chan's own power to believe is put to the test.
