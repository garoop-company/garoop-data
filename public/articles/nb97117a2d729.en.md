## Making AI Technology Familiar and Approachable

We are a startup based in Dejima, Nagasaki, working to "make AI technology more familiar and approachable."

### Why did we create Garu-chan AI?

With the arrival of generative AI such as ChatGPT, our lives and work have become dramatically more convenient. However, AI is still just a "handy tool." We want to make AI a "family who connects hearts" and connect it more deeply with people.

### The purpose of Garu-chan AI

We developed Garu-chan AI, an interactive AI from Nagasaki. This service is designed so that people and AI can connect more deeply. By making AI more familiar and approachable, we aim to deepen the relationship between people and AI.
