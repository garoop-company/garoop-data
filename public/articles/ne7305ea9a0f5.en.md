## What Is Mentality?
Mentality is the idea that action decides everything. Many people think, "I'll start once I feel motivated," but the order is actually reversed. People can only get serious through action, then emotion, then thought.

## Conditions for Getting Serious
- Take action no matter what
- Logical thinking is a tool for action

Here are some points for improving your mentality.
* Start with action first
* Value your emotions
* Treat logical thinking as a tool for action

By putting these points into practice, you can improve your mentality and achieve your goals.
