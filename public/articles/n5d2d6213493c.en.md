## What Is the 10-Year-Old Entrepreneur Training School?

The "10-Year-Old Entrepreneur Training School (Garoop School)," run by Daiki Yamashita, CEO of Garoop Inc., is a training ground where children master generative AI as a "weapon" and build both business skills and character at the same time.

### Based in Nagasaki and Fukuoka

Based in Nagasaki and Fukuoka, it is a training ground where children master generative AI as a "weapon" and build both business skills and character at the same time.

### Not Teaching, but Training

The core of the school is the idea of training children rather than teaching them. What matters is that children learn on their own and grow on their own.

### Into the Fire at 10

By entering the fire at age 10, children can gain the strongest mind and body. As a place to train character, business, and AI, Garoop School gives children great opportunities to grow.
